Brands moving fast

Four brands whose numbers justify the attention this quarter.

Iron Heart

▲ +115.7%YoY +131ppsupply gap

A small, fast-growing line of Japanese heavyweight denim and flannel. Search momentum for the Ultra Heavy Flannel and the IH-526J jacket is outrunning available listings by a wider margin than any other brand in this index.

A +131 percentage-point supply gap — the widest of any brand tracked this quarter — on heavyweight Japanese denim and flannel.

Arc'teryx

▲ +94.9%YoY +27.0ppsupply gap

Technical winter outerwear with the strongest combined growth and gap profile of any brand across all three months. The Beta AR and Alpha SV lead demand, and BEAMS collaboration shells carry outsized search interest relative to how rarely they list.

+94.9% YoY growth on a +27.0 percentage-point gap — the single largest brand opportunity in the entire November cohort.

Moncler

▲ +18.7%YoY +35.8ppsupply gap

Growth here is the slowest of the November cohort, but the gap is still wide — and Fall 2026 archive-focused releases are pulling renewed attention toward vintage Karakorum and Grenoble ski pieces specifically.

A +35.8 percentage-point gap on a brand growing at a fifth the rate of its peers — the opportunity here is archive-specific, not broad-based.

Kiton

▲ +89.1%YoY +42.7ppsupply gap

The smallest brand carried into December, and the fastest-growing one in its cohort. Holiday and formal-occasion demand is pulling hard on two-piece wool suiting specifically.

+89.1% YoY growth on a +42.7 percentage-point gap — Kiton is the smallest brand in this index growing the fastest.
